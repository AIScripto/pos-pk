# Crip Crumbs — Sales Pitch Script for Exhibitions

**Quick conversation starters and closing techniques for your sales representative**

---

## 🎯 Opening Line (15 seconds)

**For a restaurant owner/manager:**

---

### **Version A: Problem-First Approach** ✅ (Most Effective)

> "Hi! Do you manage multiple restaurant locations? If so, I'd love to show you how we're helping restaurant chains solve one problem I hear about constantly — visibility. One manager told me last week, 'I have 5 branches and I don't know which one made more sales today until I call them.' Does that sound familiar?"

---

### **Version B: Feature-First Approach**

> "Hi! We built a POS system specifically for restaurant chains. The unique thing? It works even when your WiFi is down, and you can manage all your branches from a single dashboard. Have you ever had your POS system go down?"

---

### **Version C: Value-First Approach**

> "Hi! I help restaurant chains scale from 1 branch to 100 without operational chaos. Interested in seeing how?"

---

## 🗣️ Follow-Up Questions (Understand Their Pain)

**Once you have their attention, ask:**

1. **"How many branches do you have currently?"**
   - *1-2*: Growth opportunity → pitch scaling capability
   - *3-5*: Core market → pitch central control
   - *5+*: Enterprise → pitch dedicated support

2. **"What's your biggest challenge with managing multiple locations?"**
   - If *inventory*: "We track inventory in real-time across all branches"
   - If *sales visibility*: "Real-time dashboard shows all locations"
   - If *staff coordination*: "Kitchen display system organizes orders"
   - If *WiFi issues*: "Our system works without internet"

3. **"Have you ever experienced a POS system outage?"**
   - If *yes*: "That's exactly why we built offline-first capability"
   - If *no*: "Great! That means you understand how critical reliability is"

4. **"What POS system are you using now?"**
   - Listen to their answer
   - Identify the gap
   - Position Crip Crumbs as the solution

## 💡 Key Pain Points & Responses

### **Pain Point 1: "Our current system doesn't handle multiple branches well"**

**Response:**
> "That's actually why we built Crip Crumbs. Most POS systems are designed for single locations. We're different — we're built from the ground up for multi-branch operations. You can manage all your branches from one dashboard, yet each branch operates independently. Would you like to see a quick demo?"

---

### **Pain Point 2: "Our WiFi is unreliable, and when it goes down, we lose sales"**

**Response:**
> "That's a critical pain point. Our system is offline-first — it works without internet. Orders queue locally and sync automatically when you're back online. Zero data loss. Zero transactions lost. It's like having a backup generator for your POS. That peace of mind alone is worth it."

---

### **Pain Point 3: "Managing inventory across multiple locations is a nightmare"**

**Response:**
> "Inventory is one of the biggest challenges for multi-location restaurants. With Crip Crumbs, you have:
> 1. Real-time inventory sync across all locations
> 2. Low-stock alerts
> 3. Centralized purchasing data
> 4. Branch-level analytics
> 
> One manager told me it reduced their daily stock-checking time from 2 hours to 20 minutes."

---

### **Pain Point 4: "We can't see real-time sales across our branches"**

**Response:**
> "Most owners wait until end of day to find out how sales went. With Crip Crumbs, you see real-time sales on your phone — any time, anywhere. Total sales, by branch, by product, by staff member. One franchisee told me, 'Now I know if a location is underperforming in real-time, and I can do something about it immediately instead of discovering it weeks later.'"

---

### **Pain Point 5: "Our kitchen is chaos — orders get lost, timing is wrong"**

**Response:**
> "The kitchen display system (KDS) organizes all orders on digital screens. Orders appear with priority, estimated time, and item-level details. Most customers see 30-40% improvement in order accuracy and faster service times."

---

### **Pain Point 6: "Our staff manual process is slow and error-prone"**

**Response:**
> "Paper tills and manual processes are slow and error-prone. Crip Crumbs is designed for speed — average order entry time drops from 3-4 minutes to under 1 minute. Plus, staff can use PIN-based login instead of passwords."

## 🎯 The Demo (5 minutes max)

**If they're interested, show them this flow:**

### **Demo Flow (5 steps):**

1. **Home Dashboard (30 seconds)**
   - "This is the manager dashboard. Real-time sales across all branches."
   - Point to: Total sales, number of orders, peak times
   - **Key Point**: "You see everything in one place"

2. **Multi-Branch View (30 seconds)**
   - "Here are all your branches. Each has independent operations"
   - Show: Karachi Main, Karachi North, Lahore Main, etc.
   - **Key Point**: "Central control + local autonomy"

3. **Till Operations (30 seconds)**
   - "A cashier opens their till here"
   - Show: Opening cash, shift selection, till status
   - **Key Point**: "Simple, intuitive interface"

4. **Kitchen Display System (60 seconds)**
   - "When an order is placed, it goes directly to the kitchen"
   - Show: Order queue, priority indicators, timing
   - **Key Point**: "Reduces mistakes, speeds up service"

5. **Reports & Analytics (60 seconds)**
   - "Real-time analytics on everything"
   - Show: Sales by product, staff productivity, branch comparison
   - **Key Point**: "Data-driven decisions"

## 🔐 Address the Skeptics

### **Objection 1: "We're happy with our current system"**

**Response:**
> "I hear that often. Most restaurants are content until they start growing to multiple locations — that's when pain points multiply. Most multi-location restaurants tell me, 'We didn't realize how much we were missing until we saw real-time data across all branches.' No pressure to switch now, but keep us in mind when you scale."

---

### **Objection 2: "How much does it cost?"**

**Response:**
> "Great question. It depends on your branch count. For 1-2 branches, it's affordable. For 3-10 branches, you get volume discounts. For 10+, it's custom pricing based on your specific needs. Would it help if I sent over a sample quote based on your current branch count?"

---

### **Objection 3: "Implementation sounds complicated"**

**Response:**
> "Actually, we've streamlined it to 4 weeks. Week 1 is setup and configuration, Week 2 is menu and pricing, Week 3 is hardware, Week 4 is training and go-live. We handle everything — you just provide information. Plus, we stay with you for the first month with dedicated support."

---

### **Objection 4: "What if we need custom features?"**

**Response:**
> "We build custom features for enterprise clients. Most of our base features solve 95% of use cases, but if you need something unique, we can discuss that. Let's get you on a demo call with our product team."

---

### **Objection 5: "We need to think about it"**

**Response:**
> "Totally understand. Here's what I'd suggest: take this one-pager, and if you have questions, let's do a 15-minute call with our product team — no pressure, just answer questions. Fair enough? What's the best way to reach you?"

3. **Be Patient**: Some will buy on the spot, some need time. Both are good
4. **Follow Up**: Most sales come from follow-up, not first meeting
5. **Believe in Product**: You have a genuinely good product. Confidence shows
